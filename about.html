Our story

A small team writing for curious people.

Inkwell started in 2022 as a shared notebook between three friends who were tired of clickbait. Today it's an independent publication read by thousands every week, still written by humans who care about the craft.

We publish slow, considered pieces on design, technology and living well. No ads, no trackers, just good writing.
